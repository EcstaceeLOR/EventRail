# EventRail product disclosures

## Risk and responsible trading

Event contracts are speculative and can lose all committed value. Displayed prices are market-implied estimates, not facts, financial advice, or profit guarantees. Liquidity, slippage, fees, transaction ordering, RPC failure, smart-contract bugs, and state changes between quote and inclusion can affect execution. Set a limit you can afford to lose, verify the market question and close time, and independently verify oracle evidence before claiming.

Resolved markets depend on DreamDEX contract and oracle rules. A void can pay differently from an ordinary winner. EventRail shows evidence and prepares calls but cannot change or guarantee a resolution.

## Non-custodial terms

EventRail prepares unsigned transaction data. Your wallet remains the signer and sender, and the contracts determine execution and payouts. EventRail does not hold funds, recover keys, reverse transactions, guarantee availability, or promise an outcome. You are responsible for wallet security, network fees, applicable law, taxes, eligibility, and geographic restrictions. Do not use the service where event-contract trading is prohibited.

## Privacy and retention

EventRail does not request seed phrases or private keys. It may process public wallet addresses, transaction hashes, market activity, IP/network metadata at the hosting layer, and privacy-safe product events to operate, secure, and improve the service. Integrator analytics default to 90-day retention and can be configured from 1–365 days. Security records may be retained longer where necessary. EventRail does not sell personal data or intentionally join wallet activity across integrators.

Integrators embedding EventRail may independently collect data and must provide their own notice. Requests about access or deletion should be sent through the repository support channel with no wallet secrets attached.

These disclosures are product documentation, not legal advice. Production operators must obtain jurisdiction-specific review before launch.
