# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in EventRail a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, appearance, or belief. We commit to acting in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic. Accept responsibility, focus feedback on ideas and behavior, respect privacy, and prioritize the community’s interests. Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate. Report conduct incidents privately to the repository owner through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), clearly marking the report “Code of Conduct.” Maintainers will protect the reporter’s privacy as far as possible and respond proportionately and fairly.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
